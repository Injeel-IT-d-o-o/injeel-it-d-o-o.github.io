Contact us

  • Support email: support@injeel-it.hr
  • Privacy questions: privacy@injeel-it.hr
  • Response time: we aim to reply within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, Croatian holidays excluded)

When you write, please include:

  • The email address linked to your account
  • Your device model (e.g. iPhone 15 Pro)
  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About → Software Version)
  • App version (Settings → About in the App)
  • A clear description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot
