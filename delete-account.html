This page explains how to delete your UKP Quiz account and the personal data associated with it. A Croatian version is available at injeel-it.hr/delete-account.hr.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

This is the primary method. It is authenticated, so you can only delete your own account, and it takes effect immediately:

  1. Open the UKP Quiz app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deactivated right away and your personal data is permanently deleted on the schedule described below.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email (if you can't open the app)

If you no longer have access to the app, send an email from the email address registered to your account to privacy@injeel-it.hr with the subject "Delete my account."

For your protection, we verify every request against the account's registered email before doing anything — we will reply to that address to confirm the request is genuine. We never delete an account based on an unverified message, so no one else can request deletion on your behalf. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following personal data is permanently removed:

  • Email address, username, and password hash
  • Profile details and subscription association
  • Quiz scores, answers, and completion times
  • Team membership and event registrations
  • League standings and achievements tied to your identity
  • Push notification tokens and your device identifiers

Approximate location is never stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete there.
